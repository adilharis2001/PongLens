Status: approved by Adil on 6 September (pages and wording as written;
drawn pictures; once per account; no way out except through; the three
New match doors on iOS and the Upload page on web; the tripod and
other-tables lines added to the manual sheet and the Learn guide on both

Where it lives: `src/components/RecordingBrief.tsx` and
`RecordingBriefFirstRun.tsx` on web, `RecordingBriefSheet.swift` and
`Core/RecordingBriefFirstRun.swift` on iOS, the rule in

## Why

The "Where to place the camera" sheet opens on its own twice for a new
account (see `2026-08-25-camera-guide-first-run-design.md`). It is one
tall sheet with a diagram, four rules, a landscape note and three
photographs, and a pinned Got it. People swipe it away. One pop-up with
everything on it is read by nobody; five pages with one idea each are
read by most, because each Next is a small commitment to the next idea.

The brief also carries two things the sheet never said: use a tripod, and
processing is in beta and can be wrong. The second one tempers
expectations before the first match arrives with a missed point in it.

## The pages

Each page is one picture, one title, one to four sentences, and a button.

1. **Put the camera to the side.** Side-on to the table, level with your
   half, and raised to about head height. Choose the side you do not
   serve from, so you are never standing between the camera and the
   table when a point starts. For most right-handers that is the
   forehand side. *Record doors add:* The next screen draws the table
   where it should sit. Line the real one up with it before you start.
2. **Keep the whole table in frame.** Film landscape, with every corner of
   the table in the picture and both halves clearly visible. If there are
   other tables nearby, angle the camera so they stay out of the frame
   where you can.
3. **Use a tripod.** Put the phone on a tripod or something that does not
   move, and leave it there for the whole match. A phone held in the hand
   moves, and when the picture moves PongLens loses track of where the
   table is.
4. **Don't film from behind a player.** From behind, the nearest player
   hides their half of the table, so the ball disappears exactly where it
   lands. Keep both players clear of the line between the camera and the
   table.
5. **Processing is in beta.** PongLens finds and cuts each point
   automatically, and it is not perfect yet. A point can be missed, or a
   cut can start late or run long. You can fix any point from the match
   screen, and the model improves with every release. Most matches are
   ready within 30 minutes, and we email you when yours is.

Pages 1 to 4 say Next. The last button depends on the door: Start
recording (iOS record doors), Choose a video (iOS upload), Continue (web).
Back is a text button top-left from page 2 on.

Grounding, page by page: side-on is what the pipeline is tuned for and
what the recorder's overlay draws; the corners are what table detection
needs, and at LYTTC the tracker followed the next table in 43 of 77
serves that lost their landing; calibration is pooled from sixteen frames
and assumed fixed for the match; from behind, the near player hides their
own half at the moment that decides the point; and "beta" is the word the
placement map already uses.

## The pictures

Drawn, not generated. Vector drawings in exactly the existing diagram's
palette and geometry — cyan table, magenta net, orange ball, grey players,
cyan camera — with a soft glow on the cyan and magenta strokes and a
vignetted ground, which is what lifted them from "diagram" to "picture"
after the first, flat draft. Sources are five SVGs on a 320×240 stage in
`public/brief/`. Web shows the SVG files directly; iOS gets the same files
exported to PNG at @2x and @3x by `scripts/brief/export-ios.mjs` (sharp,
already a dependency of Next — headless Chrome and QuickLook both hang on
SVG filters on this Mac), as loose files in Resources/ so `UIImage(named:)`
picks the scale. Both platforms show one set by construction. (Two
generated samples were made for comparison; the tripod one put the phone
behind a player, which is the mistake page 4 exists to prevent. Generated
pictures cannot be trusted with a precise position, and Adil left the
call to me.)

## The rule

Show the brief when all three hold:

1. this account has not completed it (`recording_brief_seen` absent or 0);
2. the account is not being back-filled;
3. the door is one of the automatic doors below.

- **Once per account**, across every door and both platforms. Five pages
  twice would be a chore, and stepping through is what makes it land.
- **Counted on completion**, when the last button is tapped, never on
  open. Abandon it (kill the app, refresh the page) and it returns from
  page 1 next time. The per-launch clause of the old rule is therefore
  unnecessary.
- **Manual opens never count.** The "How to record" trigger keeps opening
  the sheet, unchanged, everywhere it is today.
- **No way out except through.** No skip, no close, no tap outside, no
  swipe down, no Escape. Back always; swipe between pages on touch.

### Where the count lives

Two copies, higher wins, exactly as `camera_guide_seen` does: account
copy in `user_metadata.recording_brief_seen`, device copy in
`UserDefaults` / `localStorage` under `pl-recording-brief-seen:<uid>`.
The rule reuses the pure gate function with a maximum of 1 and no
session clause; the fixture table gains the new cases. The old key is
left as it is; the old automatic showing is removed, so
`CameraGuideFirstRun` on both platforms is either retired or repointed.

### Existing accounts

An account whose counter is **absent** and which has **at least one
match** is seeded straight to 1. Same reasoning and same read points as
the sheet: library load on iOS, `/upload` server render on web.

## Layout

- **iOS.** A `.large` sheet, portrait, `interactiveDismissDisabled`, no
  drag indicator. Onboarding's progress capsules in the header. Next
  pinned in a `safeAreaInset` bottom bar, `PLPrimaryButtonStyle`, full
  width, 44pt minimum. Presented from `MainTabView`'s `cameraGuideDoor`
  slot in place of `CameraPlacementSheet`; the chooser → brief → rotate
  → camera order is unchanged, and the errand is handed over in
  `onDismiss` as today.
- **Mobile web.** Full-height bottom sheet. Every page fits 393×660 with
  no scrolling; the picture is a fixed 4:3 box so the title sits at the
  same height on every page.
- **Desktop web.** Centred card, 480 wide, over the dimmed page.
  Arrow keys move, Enter presses the button, focus is trapped. Escape
  and the backdrop do nothing.
- Pages slide; `prefers-reduced-motion` switches without sliding.

## The manual sheet and the Learn guide

Two lines the brief says that they did not: use a tripod, and keep other
tables out of the frame. Both are now check rows on the sheet (web and
iOS) and a sentence in the Upload a match guide's "Record a clear video"
section, so the product says one thing in every place it talks about
filming.

Filming advice now lives in three places per platform: the brief, the
sheet, and the Learn guide. A rule added to one is added to all three,
on both platforms.

## Testing

- **iOS, a new account.** Fresh install, magic link for a throwaway
  address. Record a match → five pages → Start recording → landscape
  camera with the overlay. Back out; Record a practice session →
  nothing. Relaunch; Upload → nothing. How to record → the sheet.
- **Abandon halfway.** Page 3, kill the app, relaunch, any door → the
  brief from page 1.
- **Offline.** Network off, complete it, network on, relaunch → nothing.
- **Cross-device.** Complete on iOS, `/upload` on web, same account →
  nothing.
- **Web at both sizes.** 393×660 and desktop: no flash before the brief,
  no scrolling on any page, Escape and backdrop do nothing, refresh
  restarts at page 1.
- **The flows themselves**, to the end: a recording that starts and
  stops, an upload that completes, an upload in flight when the page
  opens, and the practice variant.
