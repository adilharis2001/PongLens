Answer "yes" for a frame ONLY if you can see at least one of these:

  - a neon sign or illuminated venue logo;
  - spectators, seating, a large or well-lit hall, several tables side by side;
  - players in matching club or national kit, or wearing numbered bibs;
  - a tripod, phone or camera visible in the shot.
