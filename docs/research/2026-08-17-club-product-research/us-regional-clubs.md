# US regional/community table tennis clubs — long-tail web presence survey

Product-discovery research for PongLens, 2026-08-17. Method: web search per region, then
WebFetch of each club's homepage (plus key pages where reachable). Famous full-time centers
excluded per brief. "Fetched" means the live site was read on 2026-08-17; a fetch failure is
itself recorded as a finding.

## Cross-club observations (detail behind the summary)

1. **Results infrastructure is absent or improvised.** Nobody in this slice has first-party
   standings. The patterns seen: external hobby trackers (Columbus and Tacoma both point at
   pong.qfwfq.org), blog-post narratives with photos (EKTT, Tacoma, KCTT), images in a slider
   (Knoxville), or nothing at all (Madison, Portland — which runs competitions three nights a
   week and publishes no outcomes). Tournament anything is outsourced to Omnipong (Columbus,
   AGTTA, KCTT, Table Tennis MN).

2. **Onboarding is cash at the door.** Nashville is literally "cash only." Madison, Baton
   Rouge, Greenville, Boulder, EKTT, Des Moines, NHTTC all collect at the door, sometimes by a
   named volunteer. Online payment exists only at Lexington (Square links), KCTT (20-day pass
   in a shop), and Harrisburg (the YMCA's RecliqueCore) — and Harrisburg's is the Y's tooling,
   not the club's.

3. **Web decay is the norm, not the exception.** Of 36 entries: two expired TLS certs (Santa
   Fe, Champlain Valley), two dead/parked domains (Omaha, Strongsville — the latter serving
   ads), one unreachable server (Dayton), one broken frameset (NATTC), one abandoned
   site-builder page (TCTTL), one login-walled Google Site (SLC), one post-redirect dead host
   (Reno), one directory frozen in 2011 (GMTTC), one permanent 2021 COVID notice (Boulder),
   and two clubs with no site at all (Ann Arbor, NHTTC).

4. **The club is one volunteer.** Contact addresses: gmail (Columbus, Milwaukee, Baton Rouge,
   Boulder, Lexington, Tacoma, DTTA, Wichita, Tulsa), yahoo (Greenville, Omaha), aol (AGTTA),
   comcast (NHTTC), infionline (Columbia). Door money goes to a named person; at Tulsa you
   phone someone inside the building to be let in; at Harrisburg you ask the desk for Suzanne.

5. **Prices and schedules hide.** Milwaukee, Knoxville, Wichita, DTTA, Denver Northside, and
   Boise publish no usable fee numbers; KCTT and Portland describe sessions without times or
   prices in places a newcomer looks. Several clubs' hours depend on a host facility's other
   bookings (Wichita warns visitors to call ahead).

6. **Video does not exist at this level.** Not one of the 30+ sites mentions match recording,
   streaming, or video review in any form — not for coaching, not for results, not for
   promotion beyond two clubs having a YouTube social icon (Tacoma, KCTT). EKTT has 31 years
   of championship lore and zero footage. The concept is simply not on the long tail's map.
